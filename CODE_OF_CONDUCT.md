# HelloWorld Society Code of Conduct 🤝

## Our Commitment

HelloWorld Society is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment for everyone interested in programming and technology.

We believe that people learn best when they feel comfortable asking questions, making mistakes, sharing ideas, and working together.

All members, contributors, organisers, and participants are expected to help maintain this environment.

---

## 🌍 Expected Behavior

Members of the HelloWorld community are expected to:

- Treat everyone with respect.
- Be welcoming to beginners.
- Listen to different perspectives.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Communicate professionally.
- Help others learn.
- Give credit to other people's work.
- Collaborate rather than discourage.
- Accept constructive criticism.
- Respect people's time and contributions.

---

## 💡 Learning Environment

HelloWorld is a learning-focused community.

Members should feel comfortable:

- Asking questions.
- Saying they don't understand something.
- Making mistakes.
- Trying new technologies.
- Sharing unfinished ideas.
- Requesting help.
- Learning from others.

Nobody is expected to know everything.

> *"There are no stupid questions when we are learning together."*

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or intimidation.
- Discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Bullying.
- Trolling.
- Hate speech.
- Deliberate disruption of community activities.
- Unwelcome sexual or inappropriate behavior.
- Sharing private or confidential information without permission.
- Taking credit for another person's work.
- Repeatedly discouraging or humiliating other members.

---

## 💻 Project Collaboration

When working on projects:

- Respect other contributors.
- Discuss disagreements constructively.
- Focus on the problem rather than the person.
- Follow project contribution guidelines.
- Give appropriate credit.
- Do not intentionally introduce harmful code.
- Respect intellectual property and licensing requirements.

---

## 🏆 Events

The same standards apply to:

- Hackathons.
- Mini bootcamps.
- Coding workshops.
- Coding challenges.
- Project showcases.
- Meetings.
- Online discussions.
- Any other HelloWorld activity.

---

## ⚠️ Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it to the HelloWorld Society committee or the designated community administrator.

Reports should include enough information for the committee to understand what happened and respond appropriately.

Reports will be handled as responsibly and confidentially as possible.

---

## 🔄 Enforcement

The HelloWorld Society committee may take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Depending on the situation, actions may include:

- A private warning.
- A request to stop the behavior.
- Temporary restriction from community activities.
- Removal from a project or event.
- Removal from the community.

Serious or repeated violations may result in stronger action.

---

## ❤️ Our Goal

The purpose of this Code of Conduct is not to restrict healthy discussion or creativity.

It exists to ensure that HelloWorld remains a place where students can:

**Learn. Build. Collaborate. Grow.**
